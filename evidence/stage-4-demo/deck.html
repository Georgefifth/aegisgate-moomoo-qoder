Why Naive LLM Trading Agents Fail in Live & Paper Markets

1. Indicator Math Hallucinations (`virattt/ai-hedge-fund` PR #759)

Open-source quant helpers frequently use Cutler's simple rolling mean instead of Wilder's exponential smoothing. Across 1,386 real Yahoo Finance bars, this caused up to 29.22 RSI points of error (on TSLA) and 235 false 30/70 threshold flips.

2. Unsettled Data Feed Glitches (`TradingAgents` PR #1707)

Market APIs can return trailing daily bars with blank `NaN` Close prices but non-zero volume (`2026-11-18,,,,,18935325`), causing LLM agents to hallucinate signals on corrupted bars.

3. Cloud-vs-Local Policy Drift (`AA Playbook` §08)

The official competition reference Playbook (§08 "Rules to Reconcile") documents 8 unresolved conflicts between cloud prompts and local desk rules (e.g. 0.5–1% vs ≤0.75% risk, 100% vs 85% exposure, weak-sector short conflicts).

4. Unprotected Paper Stops & 0DTE Forced Liquidation

Moomoo's paper API supports Limit/Market orders—not resting stop orders—and auto-exercises ITM expiring options at market close (T&C §5), triggering forced margin liquidation where Reset Cards are strictly banned.

SLIDE 09 // HONEST ENGINEERING POST-MORTEM: WHAT WORKED, WHAT FAILED & WHY 09 / 10

What Worked, What Failed During Testing & How We Fixed It

What Worked (Proven High-Leverage Decisions)

1. Separating Qualitative LLM Scouting from Deterministic Code Gating: Letting QCA agents synthesize news/anomalies while running `R01–R11` and Wilder RSI in deterministic TypeScript eliminated 235 RSI signal flips and cut 190-day drawdown by 3.4×.
2. 36% Active Buffer Under the 40% Cap (`R03`) + 3-Ticker Mandate (`R04`): Prevented single-stock concentration violations while allowing full capital deployment.
3. SGT 3-Session Isolation (`R11`): Blocking Market orders in Pre/After-Hours prevented wide-spread slippage.

What Failed in Initial Stage 3 & 3.5 Testing (And Why)

1. Zero-Volume Fallback Bug (`VAL-EDGE-ZERO-5M-VOLUME` in `initial-results.json`): Initially, `Math.max(1, floor(0 * 20 * 0.25))` defaulted to 1 share when 5m bar volume was `0`, violating Moomoo T&C §7. Fixed: Enforced `volume20xCapUnits = 0` and `R08 FAIL` when `barVolume5mShares ≤ 0`.
2. JavaScript `Math.max(0.01, NaN)` Trap (`VAL-ADVERSARIAL-NAN-INFINITY`): Corrupted `NaN` prices propagated `NaN` into share sizing. Fixed: Added strict `Number.isFinite` guards.
3. Historical Options MCP Limitation: Moomoo MCP does not yet serve historical option chains (FAQ line 911); we scoped `R09` (`DTE ≥ 2`) to live option snapshots instead of fabricating historical options backtests.
